# ( reserve * juggler ) — Autonomous Liquidity Choreography & Treasury Intelligence

> **"Protect tomorrow's capital. Optimize today's decisions. The bank does not end in the present."**

Reserve Juggler is an institutional-grade, multi-tier treasury intelligence and autonomous liquidity choreography platform. It eliminates the traditional trade-off between idle cash drag and catastrophic forced-liquidation penalties through **multi-horizon machine learning**, **convex Operations Research**, **conformal capital firewalls**, and **real-time central bank risk radar**.

## The 6 Core Strategic USPs

### 1. Adaptive Capital Allocation (Every Dollar Has a Role)
The platform continuously channels ~$20M in hourly gross transaction activity across three specialized capital zones:
- **Zone 1 — Instant Liquidity (Tier 0 Operational Cash, 0% APY)**: Immediate availability for customer payouts, clearing settlements, and net outflow spikes. Minimum reserve floor backed by P95 conformal buffers.
- **Zone 2 — Stability Yield (Tier 1 Overnight Repo & Tier 2 Sovereign Bonds)**:
  - *Tier 1 Repo ($55M @ 5.30% APY)*: 15-hour daily sweep window maturing at 08:00 UTC.
  - *Tier 2 Sovereign Bonds ($100M @ 4.85% APY)*: 1.92-year duration, evaluated with duration and interest-rate sensitivity.
- **Zone 3 — Growth Capital (Tier 3 Equities, 8.50% APY)**: Surplus capital strictly capped at 10% maximum portfolio allocation. Circuit breaker deflates exposure automatically upon macro contagion alerts.

### 2. Time-Aware Next Best Action (The Two-Hour Bridge Showcase)
Traditional treasury systems make reactive decisions when a liquidity shortfall is detected, incurring severe penalties.
- **The Problem**: At 06:00 UTC, a $10,000,000 customer payout requirement arrives. A $10M overnight repo is locked until 08:00 UTC maturity.
- **Lane A (Reactive Policy)**: Breaks the repo 2 hours early, paying a 0.5% early-exit fee ($50,000 loss).
- **Lane B (Reserve Juggler Time-Aware Engine)**: Detects that the repo matures in exactly 2 hours. Draws an intraday credit line at 12% APR:
  $$\text{Bridge Interest} = \$10,000,000 \times 12\% \times \left(\frac{2}{8,760}\right) = \$273.97$$
  At 08:00 UTC, the repo matures, and the bridge principal and $273.97 accrued interest are repaid.
  **Direct Net Saving: $49,726.03 (99.45% cost reduction).**

### 3. Hot Money vs Core Capital (Protect the Future. Don't Liquidate It.)
- **Hot Money**: Short-term obligations and unpredictable commercial flows restricted exclusively to T+0 cash and overnight repo. Zero tolerance for early-liquidation haircuts.
- **Core Capital**: True strategic surplus exceeding future obligations and stress buffers. Eligible for 4.85% sovereign bonds and capped equities.
- **The 152-Day Minimum Holding-Time Gatekeeper**: Gross simple interest takes approximately 152 days to offset a 2.0% bond exit haircut:
  $$\text{Break-Even Days} = \frac{\text{Haircut } 2.0\%}{\text{Yield } 4.80\%} \times 365 = 152.08 \text{ Days}$$
  The engine automatically **blocks** bond allocations if projected customer payouts arrive before Day 152!

### 4. Treasury Safety Score & Autonomous Capital Firewall
A technical composite health console (0–100 scale) monitoring:
- Liquidity Coverage Ratio (30% weight)
- Stress Scenario Survival (20% weight)
- Forecast Reliability & Calibration (20% weight)
- Time-to-Liquidity Alignment (15% weight)
- Forced-Liquidation Avoidance (10% weight)
- Market-Risk Safety (5% weight)
**Non-Bypassable Hard Gate Enforcer**: A high composite score *never* overrides a failed mandatory reserve floor or customer payout commitment.

### 5. Dual-Core AI: Hybrid ML + Operations Research
- **Machine Learning (Forecasting Core)**: Ridge regression with L2 regularization captures lagged flow momentum; Day-of-Week and Hour-of-Day seasonal models capture recurring corporate payroll waves.
- **Operations Research (Optimization Core)**: SciPy Sequential Least Squares Quadratic Programming (SLSQP) calculates optimal non-negative ensemble weights summing to 1.0 (`Seasonal: 96.8%`, `Ridge: 3.2%`, `Baseline: 0.0%`). Convex optimization calculates the lowest-cost feasible allocation satisfying all regulatory gates.

### 6. Macro-Panic Sentinel & Real Central Bank Intelligence
Monitors global macroeconomic contagion using verified official RSS sources:
- [Federal Reserve RSS Directory](https://www.federalreserve.gov/feeds/feeds.htm)
- [European Central Bank RSS Announcements](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/home/html/rss.sk.html)
- [Bank of Japan Official Policy Releases](https://www.boj.or.jp/en/)
- [Reuters Markets Financial Wire](https://www.reuters.com/markets/)

**Transparent 6-Factor Event Impact Formulation**:
$$\text{Impact Score} = 100 \times C \times (0.25E + 0.25R + 0.20M + 0.15V + 0.10T + 0.05N)$$
Where $C$ is source credibility, $E$ is event severity, $R$ is portfolio relevance, $M$ is market confirmation, $V$ is reporting velocity, $T$ is recency, and $N$ is negative sentiment.

Includes historical replay case studies:
1. **Japan Market Crash (August 5, 2024)**: Nikkei fell 12.4% following BOJ rate hike to 0.25% and carry-trade unwind. Reserve Juggler automatically capped Tier 3 equities from 10% to 2% and widened cash reserves by +$15M.
2. **Silicon Valley Bank (March 10, 2023)**: Rapid $42B outflow in 24 hours. Demonstrates how Hot Money insulation protects customer payouts without forced liquidation.

## Whole-System Self-Learning Lifecycle

Reserve Juggler records every telemetry event, prediction residual, human approval, and settlement outcome into SQLite:
$$\text{Observe} \longrightarrow \text{Forecast} \longrightarrow \text{Assess Risk} \longrightarrow \text{Optimize} \longrightarrow \text{Explain} \longrightarrow \text{Approve} \longrightarrow \text{Execute} \longrightarrow \text{Measure} \longrightarrow \text{Validate} \longrightarrow \text{Deploy}$$
- **Shadow Mode Evaluation**: New candidate models and policies run in shadow mode against holdout verification data.
- **Zero Unaudited Promotion**: Models are only promoted to champion status after verified outperformance, with an auditable rollback path.

## Getting Started

### Prerequisites
- Python 3.10+ (Tested up to Python 3.13)
- Modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari)

### 1. Install Dependencies
```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt
```

### 2. Run Test Suite
```bash
python -m pytest tests -q
# Output: 320 passed, 2 skipped in ~50s
```

### 3. Launch the Platform
```bash
python scripts/run_server.py --port 8089
```
Open your browser at:
```
http://localhost:8089/platform.html
```
